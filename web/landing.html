ABOUT WATERHALL

A Better Way to Manage Water Services

WaterHall is a web and offline mobile-based water meter billing and administrative records system designed to help improve water service management and make records easier to organize and access.

Community Record Keeping

Replaces error-prone manual paper logs with structured digital accounts for every household in Barangay Tagpopongan, ensuring transparent consumption records and historical billing data.

Administrative Clarity

Provides local barangay administrators with verified records of meter readings, revenue audits, registration reviews, and community-wide public announcements.

Operational Awareness

Integrates reservoir measurements to provide operational insight into reservoir water levels, turbidity, and total dissolved solids. Measurements assist local management and do not certify drinking-water safety.

WHAT WATERHALL PROVIDES

Community water records and reservoir monitoring

The system covers household billing, resident services, field meter work, and reservoir telemetry limited to water level, turbidity, and TDS.

Reservoir monitoring

ESP32 telemetry reports water level (JSN-SR04T), turbidity, and TDS. An unavailable water-level reading is shown as unavailable data, not as a low-water emergency.

Billing and resident ledger

Workers record manual meter readings. Confirmed rates apply to new bills only. Residents view unpaid bills, consumption, and payment status from stored records.

Incident reports and announcements

Residents can submit a concern with optional photo evidence. Administrators publish announcements to all users, workers, or residents.

Worker field records

After an online sign-in, the Worker app can queue meter work locally and synchronize when connectivity returns. Payments are settled in person at the Barangay Hall, not in the field.

SYSTEM & TECHNOLOGY

Designed for Community Reliability

A resilient multi-tier architecture built with modern web, offline mobile, and IoT components.

System Components

  • Admin Web Portal: Centralized dashboard for billing audits, registration reviews, and reports.
  • Worker Mobile Application: Field collection tool with SQLite offline storage and synchronization.
  • Resident Mobile Application: Household billing transparency and service concern reporting.
  • Flask Backend / API: Secure RESTful API powering core validation, auth, and data services.
  • PostgreSQL Database: Hosted cloud database for transactional records and audit history.

Deployment & IoT Scope

  • Cloud Platform: Automated continuous deployment via Vercel and GitHub.
  • Microcontroller: ESP32 low-power Wi-Fi enabled embedded controller.
  • Water Level Sensor: JSN-SR04T waterproof ultrasonic sensor for reservoir level measurement.
  • Turbidity Sensor: NOTE1 Turbidity Sensor Module for relative clarity detection.
  • TDS Sensor: Total Dissolved Solids probe for electrical conductivity estimation.
